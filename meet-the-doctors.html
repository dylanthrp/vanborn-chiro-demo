About Us · Doctors and Staff

Two Pethtels. Three decades of training. One family practice.

The clinic was opened in 1968 by the late Dr. Donald L. Pethtel, D.C., D.A.C.S. Today it is carried forward by his children — Dr. Renee I. Pethtel and Dr. Donald G. Pethtel — and a small staff that has been with the practice for years.

Source: vanbornchiropractic.com/page/doctor.html ↗

Dr. Renee I. Pethtel, B.S., D.C.

Pediatric & Prenatal Care · Webster Certified

A 1996 graduate of Life University in Marietta, Georgia, Dr. Renee writes that her own experience with chiropractic began just after birth — she was born over a month premature, and her father, the late Dr. Donald L. Pethtel, worked with her over the days before a scheduled blood transfusion; on the morning of the third day the medical team reported her function had returned to nearly normal and the transfusion was no longer needed.

She has trained under the International Chiropractic Pediatric Association (ICPA) for adjusting newborns and children of all ages, is certified in the Webster Technique for pregnant patients, and was pursuing the Certified Chiropractic Wellness Practitioner (CCWP) credential at the time of publication.

Her father opened the Van Born Chiropractic Clinic in 1968. "Over the years, we have always prided ourselves in being able to offer quality Chiropractic Healthcare at affordable rates."

Dr. Donald G. Pethtel, D.C.

U.S. Marine Corps Veteran · Life University 2001

Dr. Donald G. grew up with a chiropractor for a father, which gave him a deep appreciation for what chiropractic can do. Before beginning his formal chiropractic education he served his country by enlisting in the Marine Corps — an experience he says gave him first-hand knowledge of the physical and mental demands that can lead a person to injury and poor health.

He is a 2001 graduate of Life University in Marietta, Georgia, and previously operated clinics in East Lansing, Michigan, and Austin, Texas, before bringing his experience to the Van Born Chiropractic Clinic.
