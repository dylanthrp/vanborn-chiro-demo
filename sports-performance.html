Wellness Library · Sports & Performance

Chiropractic for athletes, weekend warriors and active lives.

How the clinic frames chiropractic care for sports performance, recovery and injury prevention. Call the clinic to discuss whether it's right for your sport.

Source: vanbornchiropractic.com/page/sports-and-chiropractic.html ↗

Start with your goals

The clinic lists sports injury care among its services. Discuss your sport, training demands, health history and what you want to return to doing. Care should begin with an individual assessment, not a promised performance gain.

Questions for your assessment

Ask what may be contributing to your symptoms, which assessments are appropriate, and whether you need evaluation by another healthcare professional. Discuss the possible benefits and risks of each option.

Recovery and return to activity

Ask how recommendations will fit with your other care and how progress will be reviewed. There is no universal recovery timeline or guaranteed improvement in athletic performance.

Preparing for the conversation

Bring a list of current treatments and questions about activity, costs and follow-up. After a significant injury, seek an appropriate medical assessment rather than relying on an article or website to decide when to return to sport.

Active and considering care?

Talk to the clinic about your sport, training load and any injuries. Ask whether an assessment is appropriate.
