Your first visit

What to expect.

A new-patient appointment at Van Born is a conversation, not a sales pitch. Three things happen, in order.

  1. 1

    You talk, they listen

    The first ten minutes are a conversation. Bring your questions, your history, and a list of anything you've tried before. The doctor is interested in the pattern of your pain, not just the spot.

  2. 2

    A focused assessment

    A short physical exam: how you move, where it hurts, what relieves it. No full-body X-rays, no pre-paid treatment packages. You'll be told what they found before anything else happens.

  3. 3

    A plan you can take or leave

    You'll leave with a written summary of findings and the doctor's recommendations. Whether you continue care at Van Born, with another clinician, or not at all — that's your call. No pressure, no contracts.

To schedule, call (313) 291-1060. The front desk knows the doctors' availability and won't push a same-day appointment if the timing isn't right.

Privacy & trust

We don't see, store, or share patient information.

This site is read-only: it links to the clinic's published phone number and forms. There is no intake form, no patient portal, no database, no analytics tracking your visit.

No intake forms

"Speak with the clinic" links you to the clinic's published phone number. The phone call is between you and the front desk — the website never sees your name, your symptoms, or your insurance.

No patient forms hosted here

"Patient forms →" opens the clinic's official PDFs from the clinic's existing website. We don't copy, store, or log them. Your printable files never touch a server we control.

No tracking, no third parties

No Google Analytics, no ad pixels, no Facebook pixel, no chat widgets, no AI tools reading content on the page. Nothing on this site tells anyone who's visiting, from which device, or how long.

Encrypted in transit

Every page is served over HTTPS (the padlock in your address bar), with a certificate that auto-renews. Nobody on a coffee-shop Wi-Fi can read what you're reading.
