What we treat

If it hurts to move,
we can probably help.

Chiropractic care for the most common musculoskeletal problems — from weekend-warrior back pain to whiplash that won't quit.

Lower back pain

Sciatica, disc problems, muscle spasm — the everyday kind and the kind that stops you cold.

Neck pain

Tech neck, stiff neck from sleep, post-accident — gentle adjustments that restore motion.

Headaches & migraines

Cervicogenic headaches, tension headaches — many cases trace back to the upper neck.

Sciatica

That shooting pain down your leg — often a pinched nerve we can free up without surgery.

Whiplash

After an auto accident — the pain often shows up days later. Early care makes a difference.

Scoliosis

Postural management and gentle adjustments to reduce pain and slow progression.

Chronic pain

Long-standing pain that's not getting better on its own. A plan, not a pill.

Wellness & prevention

Stay out of pain in the first place — regular adjustments for active Dearborn families.
