What we treat

Questions about pain
and movement?

Topics to discuss with a qualified clinician. An individual assessment is needed to determine what care is appropriate; these descriptions do not promise treatment outcomes.

Lower back pain

Sciatica, disc problems, muscle spasm — the everyday kind and the kind that stops you cold.

Neck pain

Neck stiffness and discomfort can have different causes. Ask about an assessment.

Headaches & migraines

Headaches have many possible causes. Discuss new or changing symptoms with a clinician.

Sciatica

Pain travelling down the leg needs an assessment to understand its cause and care options.

Whiplash

Seek an appropriate medical assessment for neck pain following an auto accident.

Scoliosis

Discuss spinal curvature, discomfort and suitable management options with your clinician.

Chronic pain

Persistent pain may need a broader assessment and an individual approach to management.

Wellness & prevention

Ask about movement, daily habits and care options suited to your health goals.

Meet the doctors

A family practice,
for your family.

Van Born Chiropractic was opened in 1968 by the late Dr. Donald L. Pethtel, D.C., D.A.C.S., whom Dr. Renee identifies as her father in her published biography.

Both doctors graduated from Life University in Marietta, Georgia: Dr. Renee in 1996 and Dr. Donald G. in 2001.

Biographical details and portraits are drawn from the official doctors and staff page.

Dr. Renee I. Pethtel, B.S., D.C.

Doctor of Chiropractic

A 1996 graduate of Life University. Her published biography describes postgraduate training with the International Chiropractic Pediatric Association and certification in the Webster Technique.

Dr. Donald G. Pethtel, D.C.

U.S. Marine Corps veteran

A 2001 graduate of Life University. Dr. Donald G. served in the Marine Corps before his chiropractic education and previously operated clinics in East Lansing, Michigan, and Austin, Texas.
