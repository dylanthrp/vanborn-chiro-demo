Meet the doctors

A family practice,
for your family.

"My father opened this clinic in 1968. Three generations of patients later, we're still here — still family-run, still the way he would have done it."

Van Born Chiropractic has been a fixture on Van Born Road since 1968, when the late Dr. Donald L. Pethtel, D.C., D.A.C.S. opened the doors. His children — Dr. Renee and Dr. Donald G. — carry on the practice today, joined by a small team that knows most patients by name.

Both doctors graduated from Life University in Marietta, Georgia, the same chiropractic school their father attended. The approach is unchanged: a careful diagnosis, conservative treatment, and the kind of attention you only get from a small family-run practice.

Chiropractic care without drugs or surgery — for everyday back pain, the car accident that won't quit, and the patients who have been told they just have to live with it.

Dr. Renee I. Pethtel, B.S., D.C.

Pediatric & prenatal specialist

A 1996 graduate of Life University. Dr. Renee is trained under the International Chiropractic Pediatric Association and is certified in the Webster Technique for pregnant moms. A member of the Michigan Association of Chiropractors since 1996, she has spent nearly three decades serving Dearborn Heights families.

Dr. Donald G. Pethtel, D.C.

U.S. Marine Corps veteran

A 2001 graduate of Life University. Dr. Don served in the Marine Corps before his chiropractic career, which gives him a deep understanding of the physical and mental demands that lead to injury. He previously operated clinics in East Lansing and Austin before bringing his experience home to the family practice.
