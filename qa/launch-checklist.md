# Van Born Chiropractic — demo versus production

This is an unsolicited portfolio concept, not an authorized clinic website. Visual polish and automated test passes do not establish medical, legal, privacy, or operational readiness.

## Required before an official launch

- Clinic approval of domain, design, branding, staff identities/current roles, address, hours and all copy.
- Confirm permission to reuse headshots and patient testimonials. Public availability and attribution alone do not prove permission.
- Clinician review of descriptions of conditions/treatments; do not promise outcomes or imply chiropractic treats autism, asthma or other unsupported conditions.
- Verify existing vendor contract and ownership/licensing of content, library articles, domain and downloadable forms. Preserve URLs or plan redirects; do not copy vendor-owned articles without permission.
- If appointment requests are enabled later: clinic-owned receiving system, delivery/failure tests, clear request-versus-confirmed wording, minimal data collection, privacy policy, access/retention controls, and applicable healthcare privacy review. No patient information should be collected by the current portfolio demo.
- Confirm hours (including alternating Saturdays and holidays) directly with clinic, not only an aging website.
- Test actual click-to-call on a phone and directions in the user's map app. Browser URL checks are not completed calls.
- Verify final public deployment, content, images and mobile interactions after publishing. Local tests do not prove the live release changed.
