Forms to bring to your first visit.

Save time at your first appointment by completing these forms ahead of time. PDFs open on the clinic's official website; we do not collect or store them here.

Six documents to bring or review

All links open the clinic's original PDFs on their official website. The form descriptions below are summarized; please read each form in full before signing.

  1. New Patient Form

    The standard intake form for a first-time visit. Includes basic contact information, health history and the reason for your visit.

    Open PDF ↗

  2. Inactive / Clinic Re-Entry form

    For patients whose last visit was more than six months ago. Updates your file before scheduling again.

    Open PDF ↗

  3. Neck Disability Index

    A short questionnaire used to track how neck pain affects your daily activities. Useful to fill out before any visit focused on neck or upper-back symptoms.

    Open PDF ↗

  4. Oswestry Disability Index

    A short questionnaire used to track how low-back pain affects your daily activities. Useful to fill out before any visit focused on back or leg symptoms.

    Open PDF ↗

  5. HIPAA Policy

    The clinic's privacy policy describing how your health information is used and protected under federal HIPAA rules.

    Open PDF ↗

  6. Good Faith Estimate

    Required under the No Surprises Act. You have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate explaining the expected charges for your care. Ask the clinic for the current estimate at your visit or by phone.
