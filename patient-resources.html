Forms to bring to your first visit.

Ask the clinic which forms apply to your visit before completing them. Document links open on the clinic’s website; completed forms cannot be submitted through this preview.

Six documents to bring or review

Where a document link is available, it opens the clinic’s original PDF. Read each document in full and ask the clinic how to return completed forms securely. Ask the clinic directly for a current Good Faith Estimate.

  1. New Patient Form

    The standard intake form for a first-time visit. Includes basic contact information, health history and the reason for your visit.

    Open PDF ↗

  2. Inactive / Clinic Re-Entry form

    For patients whose last visit was more than six months ago. Updates your file before scheduling again.

    Open PDF ↗

  3. Neck Disability Index

    A short questionnaire used to track how neck pain affects your daily activities. Useful to fill out before any visit focused on neck or upper-back symptoms.

    Open PDF ↗

  4. Oswestry Disability Index

    A short questionnaire used to track how low-back pain affects your daily activities. Useful to fill out before any visit focused on back or leg symptoms.

    Open PDF ↗

  5. HIPAA Policy

    The clinic's privacy policy describing how your health information is used and protected under federal HIPAA rules.

    Open PDF ↗

  6. Good Faith Estimate

    Required under the No Surprises Act. You have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate explaining the expected charges for your care. Ask the clinic for the current estimate at your visit or by phone.

Before you visit

Questions patients ask.

Practical answers to help you plan your next step.

How do I arrange an appointment?

Call (313) 291-1060 to ask about availability. This preview cannot request, reserve or confirm an appointment.

What should I prepare for my first visit?

Ask the clinic which forms to complete and what documents to bring. Prepare your questions and a list of medications and prior care to discuss directly with your clinician. View patient resources.

Is my insurance accepted, and what will care cost?

Fees, insurance participation and individual coverage need to be confirmed directly with the clinic and your insurer. Ask about expected charges before deciding on care. This site does not quote prices or guarantee coverage.

When is the clinic open?

The clinic publishes Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday hours of 9:00 AM–6:00 PM, and 9:00 AM–12:00 PM on the 1st, 3rd and 5th Saturdays when applicable. Sunday and Tuesday are listed as closed. All times are local (Eastern). Call to confirm holiday hours and appointment availability. View hours and directions.

Can I send health information through this website?

No. There is no intake form or patient portal here. Contact the clinic about an appropriate way to share health, insurance or payment information. Read about this preview and privacy.

Can these articles tell me which care I need?

No. Library titles and patient experiences are not a diagnosis or a promise of results. Ask a qualified clinician about your symptoms and care options. For a medical emergency, call 911 rather than waiting for a clinic response.

Ready to schedule?

Call the clinic and they'll tell you which forms to bring, what to expect, and whether your insurance is accepted.
